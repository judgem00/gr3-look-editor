Parts of tools/ramload follow GRKit (https://github.com/ahonn/grkit):

- the ModeSet commands, the bytes of DEVELOP.MOD and the ModeSet file names
  (crates/grkit-core/src/runtime.rs, crates/grkit-core/src/camera.rs)
- card/dump.ttl and card/load.ttl (runtime/dump/STARTUP.TTL)
- the syscall wrapper, the _start stub, the compiler flags and the layout of
  dump.c (runtime/dump/dump.c, crates/grkit-build/src/dump.rs)
- the way load.c writes: /dev/mem with O_SYNC, dsb and cacheflush after each
  range, roll-back on failure (runtime/installer.c)
