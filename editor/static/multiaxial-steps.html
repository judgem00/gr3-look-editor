MultiAxial steps

The camera's hue-selective stage splits the Cb/Cr plane into four quadrants at fixed axes. Within each quadrant, up to five steps have bounds that can move freely; each step applies one fixed correction. Drag the bounds, or let the fit place them. The curves are examples, and the fit is simplified: unlike the editor, it neither merges steps down to 11 corrections nor snaps bounds onto the axes. See multiaxial.md.
